Our story

Built from scratch,
driven by iteration.

Firebytes is the robotics team at BASIS Phoenix, now in our second season. Our rookie year started with almost nothing: a goBILDA starter bot, a box of donated structural parts from a team that never competed, and a group of students who had never built a competition robot before.

We replaced the starter drivetrain with mecanum wheels, then rebuilt the intake, launcher, and electronics around it. Parts delays and registration hurdles cost us months, so we compressed the entire build into the two weeks before our first qualifier — and finished the robot the morning of the event.

“We won the Judges’ Choice Award and qualified for the State Championship in our rookie season.”

Since then we have kept iterating: new odometry pods, a Limelight camera for autonomous alignment, a redesigned single-shaft intake, and a rebalanced control scheme based on what our drivers learned under match pressure. This season we are building on all of it.
