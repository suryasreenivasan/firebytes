Outreach

Robotics does not stop at our build room.

Our mission is to spread awareness for robotics and STEM. In our rookie season we had a lot to learn — and a lot to give back.

📚

FTC Kickoff CAD workshop

We hosted an outreach event at FTC Kickoff teaching CAD basics for FTC — introducing roughly 60 students to what CAD is and how to model in Onshape, many of them complete novices.

~60 studentsOnshapeBeginners
🧩

Mentoring FLL 62351

We work with the middle school FIRST LEGO League team at our school, helping with robot construction and programming while building a pathway from middle school into high school robotics.

🤝

Learning from veterans

Visits to established programs at BASIS Goodyear and Brophy College Preparatory gave us technical guidance and inspiration we could not have found on our own.

📣

Building a presence

Our social media documented member spotlights, build progress, and mentoring moments — reaching students across BASIS Phoenix and beyond.

🌱

Three future members

We recruited three incoming members and have already taught them CAD modeling, the REV Hardware Client, and hands-on robot modifications.

💰

Sustainable funding

We track every purchase in a categorized spreadsheet and are building fundraising capacity so the team outlasts its founding members.
