Join the team

Want to build The Phoenix 2.0?

No experience required. Most of our founding members had never touched a competition robot before our rookie season.

How to join

Email us and we will send you everything you need to get started.

  1. Send an email to suryasreenivasan0@gmail.com
  2. Get instructions — we will reply with next steps, forms, and what to expect.
  3. Come to a meeting and see what we are working on.
Email us to join →

Meetings

We usually meet over the weekends at a team member’s house. Build sessions run long when a competition is close — bring snacks and a willingness to redo things.

WeekendsTeam member’s houseAll skill levels

What you will learn

CAD in Onshape, fabrication and assembly, Java programming for FTC, autonomous routines with odometry and vision, and how to run a team like a small engineering organization.

Support us

Help us keep building.

Robotics is expensive: control hubs, motors, cameras, and structural parts add up fast. Our rookie season ran about $3,300 against a $2,500 budget, and a $1,000 grant from the Argosy Foundation helped close the gap. Every donation goes straight into parts, registration fees, and travel to competitions — and into keeping this team alive for the students who come after us.
